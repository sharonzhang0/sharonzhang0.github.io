Sharon Zhang

Bio

Sharon attends Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health as a part-time MPH student concentrating in Epidemiology and Biostatistics. She also works as a Senior Product Manager at CVS Health, where her role focuses on optimizing use of external claims data across lines of business (Aetna, Retail, Specialty, Caremark, Healthspire).

Previously, she worked as a Senior Solutions Manager at Flatiron Health, where she led knowledge management, customer success, and feature rollouts for the Real-World Evidence (RWE) business in support of cancer researchers, and as a Consultant at Simon-Kucher, where she delivered go-to-market pricing and market access strategies to support drug launches across 10+ countries. She also holds a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from the University of Chicago.

Sharon is passionate about leveraging data and technology to improve health outcomes at scale, with particular interest in the areas of oncology and mental health.

Projects

  • CHAI: Cleaned and analyzed patient chart-abstracted data to evaluate programmatic impact of the Nigeria Cancer Access Partnership (NCAP) on patient access to quality, affordable drugs.

  • Crisis counseling: Conducted exploratory analysis to evaluate the real-world performance and impact of deploying an LLM-based copilot at scale in a WhatsApp-based crisis counseling service in India.

  • ICER evaluation: Replicated an Institute for Clinical and Economic Review (ICER) cost-effectiveness analysis of non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) therapies using electronic health record data from Flatiron Health.
